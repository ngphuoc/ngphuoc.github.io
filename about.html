About Phuoc Nguyen

Research Lecturer at the Applied Artificial Intelligence Institute (A2I2), Deakin University, Australia.

Academic biography

Phuoc Nguyen is a Research Lecturer at the Applied Artificial Intelligence Institute (A2I2) of Deakin University, Australia. He earned his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Canberra, Australia, where he focused on machine learning and kernel methods.

His current research spans causality and graphical causal models, Bayesian deep learning, large language models (LLMs) and stochastic differential equations (SDEs), with applications in causal discovery, inference and root cause analysis.

He applies his methods across health and medical records, time series analysis, network systems, data streaming, deep network compression, experimental design, materials discovery and reinforcement learning. He is developing scalable machine learning and causal methods for these areas, while addressing emerging challenges arising from real-world systems.

Selected project collaborations

  • Optimisation of certification methodologies for additively manufactured aircraft components using artificial intelligence (2025–2026).
  • Tree-canopy extraction from high-resolution satellite imagery (2025).
  • Machine learning for rapid materials design and assessment (2021–2024).
  • AI algorithmic assurance and AI system evaluation (earlier collaborative projects).
