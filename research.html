Research

Research at the intersection of learning, causality and dynamics

My research spans causality and graphical causal models, Bayesian deep learning, large language models (LLMs) and stochastic differential equations (SDEs), with applications in health, science and engineering.

01

Causality and Bayesian deep learning

Developing graphical causal models and Bayesian learning methods for causal discovery, inference and root cause analysis. I study how uncertainty affects learned structures and explanations, and how these methods can scale to complex real-world data.

Causal discovery & inferenceGraphical causal modelsBayesian deep learningRoot cause analysis

Selected papers include ICDM 2025, AAAI 2024 and ICDM 2023.

02

Language models and stochastic dynamics

Studying large language models alongside learning methods for systems that evolve over time. My interests include stochastic differential equations, time series analysis, causal dynamics and reasoning under uncertainty, as well as reinforcement learning and interacting agents.

Large language modelsStochastic differential equationsTime series & causal dynamicsReinforcement learningMulti-agent learning

Related work on interacting agents includes Memory-Augmented Theory of Mind Network (AAAI 2023). See the full research record for further outputs.

03

AI for health, science and engineering

Applying machine learning and causal methods to health and medical records, network systems, streaming data and materials discovery. My work also spans experimental design, deep network compression and AI assurance, with an emphasis on scalable methods that address practical challenges.

Health & medical recordsNetwork systems & data streamsExperimental designMaterials discoveryAI assurance

Selected collaborations are documented in the Deakin grant and contract records.
