Prospective PhD students

Potential research directions

Projects may be developed around causal discovery and inference, Bayesian deep learning, large language models, stochastic differential equations and causal dynamics, or applications in health, science and engineering.

These are indicative research interests, not confirmed funded openings.

What to include in your enquiry

  1. A brief description of your research interests and a question you want to address.
  2. Your CV and academic background.
  3. One or two relevant papers or projects, and your own contribution.
  4. Any research writing or code you would like to share.
  5. Whether you seek a funded PhD position or have independent funding.

Availability: This website does not currently advertise a confirmed scholarship or vacancy. Enquiries about supervision fit are welcome, but funding and admission depend on Deakin's applicable processes.
